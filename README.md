# Wingman

**Your engineering day, in one window.**

Wingman is a native macOS dashboard that pulls together the things you'd otherwise have twelve browser tabs open for — pull requests, tickets, and build status — so you can see where your work actually stands without going looking for it.

## What it does

- **Repositories** — Keep an eye on your repositories and their pull requests, so nothing slips past on your watch.
- **Users** — Track your teammates' pull requests and be the reviewer they didn't know they needed.
- **Notifications** — Hear about new and merged pull requests, and get a nudge when it's your turn to review.
- **Issues** - Pull requests are automatically linked to their issues, so you always know what a change is actually for.

Built in Swift, native on the platform, no Electron in sight.
