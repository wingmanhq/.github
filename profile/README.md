## WingmanHQ

Wingman is a native Mac app that watches your GitHub repos and teammates, and tells
you the moment a pull request needs you. It’s nearly ready!

### Built for the review you keep forgetting.
Four features, one app, zero browser tabs.

#### Repositories
Keep an eye on your repositories and their pull requests, so nothing slips past on your watch.

#### Users
Track your teammates’ pull requests and be the reviewer they didn’t know they needed.

#### Notifications
Hear about new and merged pull requests, and get a nudge when it’s your turn to review.
